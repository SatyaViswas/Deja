# 🎬 Déjà 3-Minute Video Presentation Script

This script is highly optimized to fit within 3 minutes. It relies on the Oracle to dynamically fetch your past seeded dates, so you don't need to memorize random timestamps. This makes the demo look incredibly authentic and powerful.

---

## ⏱️ 0:00 - 0:45 | Introduction & The Slate (Data Ingestion)
**Goal:** Show the clean UI and how effortlessly data enters the Cognee graph.

1. **Start on The Slate (Journal) Tab.**
2. **Action:** Type a brand new entry live: *"I've been feeling immense pressure about my placement interviews lately, but I really loved playing rugby yesterday to blow off steam."*
3. **Voiceover/Explain:** *"Have you ever wished your memory came with a search engine? Welcome to Déjà, a personal memory recovery companion that acts as your external brain. To build this, we implemented a hybrid architecture: we store journals chronologically in a relational database for exact timelines, and concurrently build a semantic knowledge graph and vector DB via Cognee. To get the best of both worlds, I built the model to dynamically utilize Cognee Open Source with SQLite and LanceDB for fast, private local graph processing, and Cognee Cloud for enterprise-scale hosted graphs, connected seamlessly via a dynamic device-code authentication flow. When I hit submit here on the Slate, the Node.js API Gateway proxies this to our Python FastAPI bridge. Behind the scenes, the Cognee engine instantly structures this text, classifies the entities (like 'interviews' and 'rugby'), and wires them into my personal semantic graph."*

---

## ⏱️ 0:45 - 1:30 | The Oracle (Semantic Retrieval & Proof)
**Goal:** Prove the engine can recall multi-year patterns and show the feedback loop.

1. **Switch to The Oracle Tab.**
2. **Action - Prompt 1 (Positive Pattern):** Ask the Oracle, *"What happens to my coding performance when I do morning meditation?"*
   * **Expected AI Answer:** It will mention that you achieve a high-focus flow state and easily solve complex algorithmic problems.
3. **The Proof:** 
   * **Voiceover:** *"Because we seeded 3.5 years of random data, I don't even remember when I meditated. But the Oracle does."* 
   * *If your UI shows the date/context, point to it.* Alternatively, say: *"The Oracle didn't hallucinate this; it traversed the vector graph to find the exact historical correlations from past years."*
4. **Action - Prompt 2 (Negative Pattern):** Ask, *"How does eating junk food for lunch affect me?"*
   * **Expected AI Answer:** Severe afternoon brain fog and skipped coding sessions.
5. **Action - Show Feedback:** Click the **Thumbs Up** (Helpful) button on the Oracle's response.
   * **Voiceover:** *"By giving feedback, we reinforce the semantic weights of these graph nodes, making future recalls even more accurate."*

---

## ⏱️ 1:30 - 2:15 | Blindspots (Contradiction Detection)
**Goal:** Explain how Cognee's structural graph detects gaps in chronological data.

1. **Switch to the Blindspots Tab.**
2. **Action:** Show the Paradox Alerts/Blindspots generated by the system.
   * *Note: Based on your `oracle_manifest`, you have heavily seeded data about playing sports ("football", "basketball", "picket ball") and watching movies ("Interstellar", "Inception").*
3. **Explanation:** 
   * **Voiceover:** *"Here is the most powerful feature. Déjà compares my chronological timeline in Supabase against the structural density of my Cognee knowledge graph. It detected a Blindspot: I used to talk constantly about playing football and watching high-concept movies like Interstellar, but I haven't logged anything about them recently. The engine automatically identifies these abandoned habits and contradictions."*

---

## ⏱️ 2:15 - 2:45 | Memory Safeguard & Dissolve (Right to be Forgotten)
**Goal:** Demonstrate graph pruning.

1. **Action:** Click the **Dissolve/Forget** button on one of the Blindspots (e.g., the rugby memory you just created, or an older football memory).
2. **Explanation:**
   * **Voiceover:** *"Privacy is paramount. If I want Déjà to forget a habit, I click Dissolve. This doesn't just delete a row in a database. It triggers `cognee.forget()` in the backend, completely severing the semantic edges and pruning the vector space. The AI literally loses the ability to reason about this topic ever again."*

---

## ⏱️ 2:45 - 3:00 | Bookmarks & Conclusion
**Goal:** Wrap up with pinned insights.

1. **Switch to the Bookmarks Tab.**
2. **Action:** Show your pinned important insights.
3. **Explanation:** 
   * **Voiceover:** *"While we can dissolve what we want to hide, we can bookmark our most crucial realizations. By bridging chronological storage with both Cognee Open Source and Cognee Cloud, Déjà offers a unified, secure, and production-ready memory recovery engine."*

---

### 💡 Pro-Tip for the Judges
Since the seeded dates (`seed_stress_test.py`) are randomly assigned across 3.5 years for each user, **you do not need to memorize dates for the video**. Relying on the Oracle to instantly pull the pattern from the past is actually a *stronger* demonstration of the app's retrieval capabilities than navigating to a specific date manually!
